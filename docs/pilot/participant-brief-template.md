# Participant brief template

This template is not an approved consent form. The project lead and the applicable
ethics/academic reviewer must approve and version the final participant-facing material
before recruitment or collection. Signed forms and participant contact details must
remain outside this repository.

## Study purpose

You are invited to help evaluate a local continuous-authentication prototype. The
prototype estimates whether keyboard timing and mouse movement patterns remain
consistent with an enrolled participant. Participation is voluntary.

## What the prototype records

- broad, content-free keyboard classes and press/release timing;
- mouse movement coordinates, buttons, and scroll distances;
- a generic foreground process name/category, input-device class, screen resolution,
  and DPI; and
- collector health and data-quality counters.

It is designed not to record typed characters, raw key identifiers, reconstructed text,
window titles, document names, file paths, URLs, clipboard contents, screenshots, or
screen contents.

## What participation involves

The approved study schedule, collection duration, verification prompts, and any

## Controls and withdrawal

The operator will demonstrate the pause control before collection. Pausing stops input,
context, and device-event collection while retaining a content-free heartbeat that says
collection is paused. Explain the approved withdrawal deadline, how withdrawal is
requested, and what happens to data already included in an immutable evaluation freeze.

## Data handling and risks

Only a pseudonymous participant identifier is used in the collection database. Describe
the approved storage location, access list, retention period, deletion procedure, and
foreseeable privacy, inconvenience, reauthentication, and false-alert risks. Do not
promise perfect anonymity, accuracy, or security.
